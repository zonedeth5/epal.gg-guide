## 2. Source-by-source disposition

### 2.1 Béal & Grégoire — VERIFIED; CLAIM MAPPING RESTRICTED

Publisher record confirms:
- Mathieu Béal and Yany Grégoire.
- “How Do Observers React to Companies’ Humorous Responses to Online Public Complaints?”
- *Journal of Service Research*, 25(2), 242–259.
- First published online 4 February 2021; issue published 2022.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1177/1094670521989448.

The study combines a field study of humorous company replies on social media with scenario-based experiments. It supports a context-bounded claim about observers' responses to affiliative versus aggressive humor in public complaint-recovery situations. It does not study reply latency or establish that faster replies cause better relationships.

**Disposition:** keep in humor context; exclude from response-speed evidence.

### 2.2 Lin et al. — VERIFIED; CLAIM MAPPING RESTRICTED

### 2.5 Guang et al. — CORRECTION NOTICE NOW RETRIEVED

Publisher correction:
- Yang Guang, Sho Sakurai, Takuya Nojima, and Koichi Hirota.
- “Correction: Guang et al. Visual Harmony Between Avatar Appearance and On-Avatar Text: Effects on Self-Expression Fit and Interpersonal Perception in Social VR.”
- *Informatics*, 13(10), 165 (6 October 2026).
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/informatics13100165.

The correction explicitly identifies errors in numerical processing/reporting, the mediation specification for a paired within-participant design, the condition-group description, and failure to disclose Q13 overlap between the self-expression-fit and interpersonal-affinity composites. It says the corrected analysis was rebuilt from preserved source questionnaire responses, while some intermediate analysis files could not be reconstructed exactly.

The correction retains the main direct finding that congruent avatar–text pairings increase self-expression fit and interpersonal affinity in the tested conditions (N=21). It narrows the indirect pathway interpretation because of sensitivity to the affinity-composite specification and states that the condition-group analysis does not confirm immersion moderation.

**Disposition:** prior retrieval blocker closed. Retain the small N, VRChat setting, shared-item caveat, sensitivity analysis, and no-confirmatory-H3 limitation. Do not state full mediation or confirmed immersion moderation.
